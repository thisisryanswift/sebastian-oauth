Sebastian privacy notice

Sebastian is a private personal assistant with exactly one user: its owner and operator, Ryan Swift. It is not offered to the public and has no public signup.

These public pages provide application information and privacy disclosures for the owner's Google OAuth connection, not a public assistant service. This notice describes the intended personal Google integration; it does not expand the permissions shown by Google or authorize unrequested actions.

Google data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the assistant can access account identity and Google data needed for personal assistance:

  • Gmail messages, threads, headers, labels and relevant attachments.
  • Calendar information, events, invitations, attendees and response status.
  • Drive file metadata and relevant file contents.
  • Google Docs contents and document changes.

The selected Google scopes grant broad read/write capabilities, including sending mail, managing events and modifying files. Drive access can cover multiple file types. Data collection and actions must still follow the owner's requests, approved monitoring boundaries and account permissions.

Why the data is used

The assistant uses relevant data to answer the owner's questions, prepare briefings, connect context, track commitments and surface useful concerns. It can also send mail, create invitations and edit files or documents when the owner requests or approves the specific action. Connected content is evidence for a task, not authority to change the assistant's instructions.

Processing and sharing

Hermes, gog and the owner's self-managed systems perform assistant and Google-tool operations. Relevant excerpts, prompts and tool results may be sent to Google (Gemini), OpenAI and Anthropic, depending on the assistant's configured services. Those providers process data under the applicable service terms and account settings.

Data use, retention and model-training practices depend on the selected service, its terms and account settings. This notice makes no local-only, zero-retention or no-training guarantee.

Requested actions may disclose content to their intended recipients, event participants or existing document collaborators through Google's services. A Google access grant is not permission to publish private content on this site.

Credential custody and stored records

After initial Google consent, access and refresh credentials are held in the owner's self-managed Agent Vault, which is designed to encrypt stored credentials and refresh access as needed. The one-time authorization process uses temporary workstation files; its cleanup does not automatically remove the owner's original downloaded OAuth-client file.

Relevant source data or derived context may be retained in private assistant history, operational logs, notes and memory systems. The public static pages are not intended to receive Google credentials, mail, calendar records or documents.

Retention and deletion

Private assistant records may be kept for ongoing use. No fixed automatic deletion period or immediate erasure of backups is promised.

Revoking Google access stops future access through that authorization but does not itself delete information already retained in private histories, notes, logs, memory, backups or AI-provider systems. Retention and deletion depend on the actual systems used; AI-provider retention is subject to the applicable service terms and account settings.

Owner controls

The owner can remove this application's access in Google Account connections. The owner can also remove its credential from the self-managed vault and separately review or delete retained assistant records. Removing access may disable features that need the connection.

Google API requirements

The integration must be operated consistently with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements. Publishing this notice does not replace checking that the actual processing and provider settings meet those requirements.

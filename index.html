Sebastian

Sebastian is a private personal assistant with exactly one user: its owner, Ryan Swift. It is not offered to the public and has no public signup.

These public pages provide application information and privacy disclosures for the owner's Google OAuth connection. They are not an invitation to use the assistant or connect another person's account.

What it does

Sebastian helps the owner understand correspondence, prepare for meetings, follow commitments and work with personal documents. Its planned Google integration is designed to use the owner's consent to access:

  • Gmail: read and search correspondence, and compose, send or organize mail when requested or approved.
  • Google Calendar: review schedules and invitations, and create or update events when requested or approved.
  • Google Drive: find and read relevant files, and create or modify files when requested or approved.
  • Google Docs: read and edit documents when requested or approved.

The connection grants technical capabilities; it is not blanket permission to send messages, invite people, edit documents or delete data autonomously. Background monitoring is intended to gather context and notify the owner, not make unrequested changes.

How access is handled

The planned integration uses Hermes and gog for assistant and Google-tool functionality. Following one-time Google consent, Google credentials are held by the owner's self-managed Agent Vault. Relevant content may also be processed by the AI services configured for the assistant: Google (Gemini), OpenAI and Anthropic.

See the privacy notice for data use, storage, processing and access controls. These pages do not host the Google sign-in callback, credentials, mailbox content or private assistant records.
